encrypted messages as plain prose, on an address you derive from a passphrase ← demo
How it works. A board lives at an npub derived from a passphrase. Your message is encrypted and encoded into Glossia prose, signed, and published to public nostr relays. The site stays static — anyone opens a board by putting its #npub in the URL. The passphrase is the credential: with it you can post and decrypt; with only the npub you can read the prose but not decrypt it.

One passphrase is the whole credential: it derives the board's npub and encrypts the message. Anyone you give it to can read and post.

Share the decrypt passphrase with readers; keep the publish key to stay the only poster. (A broadcast / newsletter board.)

Glossia is a readable encoding, not steganography. AES-CTR gives confidentiality, not authentication. Boards are public on nostr; a weak passphrase is offline-guessable. Generated passphrases target ≥128 bits.
